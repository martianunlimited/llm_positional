How Transformers Understand Word Order:
Sinusoidal Waves to Modern RoPE

Self-Attention by itself is permutation invariant: it treats sentences like an unordered bag of words. Without positional encoding, "dog bites man" and "man bites dog" look mathematically identical to an LLM! Explore the ingenious mathematics that inject word sequence: from multi-frequency harmonic waves to Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) rotating vectors in 2D complex planes.

Modern RoPE: 2D Complex Unit Circle Rotations

Used in LLaMA 1/2/3, Mistral, Gemma, and Qwen: Instead of adding vectors, RoPE multiplies $Q$ and $K$ by 2D orthogonal rotation matrices. Because rotations preserve lengths ($\|R_m \vec{q}\| = \|\vec{q}\|$), the dot product strictly reflects relative distance!

📚 Architectural Evolution & Research Citations

The Evolution of Positional Representations

In original recurrent neural networks (RNNs), sequential order was inherently preserved by step-by-step state propagation. When Vaswani et al. introduced the Transformer, computation became 100% parallel, but lost all temporal sequence. Positional encodings restored this capability without sacrificing parallelization speed.

"Attention Is All You Need" (2017)

Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer et al. (Introduced Sinusoidal Positional Encoding).

"RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding" (2021)

Jianlin Su, Yu Lu, Shengfeng Pan, Bo Wen, Yunfeng Liu (Invented RoPE).
